For two decades — May 2006 through my retirement in May 2026 — I've led technical operations and small teams in U.S. Army aviation. Keeping aircraft mission-ready, running standardized procedures, and making decisions under conditions where getting it wrong can cost a life.

"Software engineering isn't a pivot. It's the same job — diagnose the problem, ship the fix, document the why — with a different toolkit."

I'm finishing my undergraduate business degree at American Public University System while preparing to start a 15-week software engineering apprenticeship through Apprenti, with on-the-job training at Visa in Austin starting June 1st.

What I bring to an engineering team is what I brought to the flightline: I stay calm when things break, I fix the problem, I don't add to it.
